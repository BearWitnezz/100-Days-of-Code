Day 9 proof project — Contractor Bid Board. A dictionary of contractor bids, a while loop with a flag to collect bids, and a find-the-highest loop over the keys using bid[contractor]. The winner is printed after the loop.

`contractor_bid_board_rebuild.py` is John’s from-memory rebuild of the Day 9 bid board, written with no help.
